### Recipe

##### Chicken

- 1 frozen skinless boneless breast, thawed
- 30min-2hr before dinner time, chop into bite size pieces
- Toss in a bowl with a few tbs of olive oil, some salt, garlic powder, onion powder to taste
- Drench a few bamboo skewers on a plate so they don't burn on the grill
- Load chicken onto skewers, they can be squished, it's okay
- Grill or Air fry for 8 min at 400F, then flip and 8 more minutes

##### Peanut Sauce

- Index card recipe calls for 1.5 cups coconut milk, I use 1/4 cup. It makes enough for two people.
  These portions are for 1/4 cup coconut milk, scale accordingly:
- 2 tbs peanut butter
- 2 tbs water
- 1 tsp yellow curry power
- 2 tsp sugar
- 1/3 tsp soy sauce
- 1/4 tsp salt

- Mix all in a saucepan, simmer on med/low until thick

##### Mango Sticky Rice

- Rinse 1/2 cup glutinous rice in a glass bowl until water runs clear, then leave covered with water (about 1/3 cup,
  water just has to be covering all the rice)
- Add 1-1.5 cup water to bottom of instant pot, place bowl on top of trivet if fits, otherwise it can go directly in the
  pot.
- Cook on high pressure for 13 min
- Natural release 10 min

While that's cooking, simmer on the stove:

- 2/3 cup coconut milk
- 2.5 tbsp sugar
- .25tsp salt.
  Keep warm on stove

After the 10 min natural release, mix coconut milk mixture into the rice and let sit to absorb 5-20 min. It's ready when
it's thick

Dice a Mango and put it on top
