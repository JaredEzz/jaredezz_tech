## Intro

This past April, some friends and I took turns picking a weekly prompt that we all wrote about.

I didn't write my last article because of excuses — my wife was sick, our baby wasn't sleeping well, and I had poor time management. I formally apologized in our biweekly game night, and shared what my answer to the question would be, so the guys forgave me. Here's my wrap-up though as a peace offering.

[Sam's wrap-up](https://samwarnick.com/blog/april-blogging-challenge-wrap-up/) went up in April and I borrowed liberally from his format, but I wanted a single matrix instead of a section per week, because I like tables.

## Things I Love About Each of Their Blogs

### [samwarnick.com](https://samwarnick.com)

One thing I love about Sam's: he thinks about design way more than anyone I know. He's like Jony Ive but not pretentious. His [CSS Pokémon cards](https://samwarnick.com/blog/my-new-pokemon-page/) were probably the first time I encountered his own flavor of design, but also just him popping up at the end of each blog post. Genius.

### [catskull.net](https://catskull.net)

Dave's is awesome in that it's so freaking random, man. You could find old stories of adventures from when he was in high school, random facts about microcontrollers or setting up encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS on your router, or tips on not adding salt to your beans while you're cooking them. He's led an interesting life and is Jack of all trades, master of all trades too. Idk how he does it. Must have some crazy good genes.

### [carter.works](https://carter.works)

Carter's is so cool because he's one of the most intelligent people i think I've ever met. And you might not think that would show through his blog's [home page](https://carter.works/), but it makes me feel dumb in comparison because I literally don't know how to find his posts. Oh yeah. I have to put `/blogs` in the URL. Or just use his [RSS feed](https://carter.works/blog/feed.xml) in his [RSS reader](https://github.com/carterworks/rss-reader) he *made*. *himself*.

Jokes aside, I love his posts because they always give me something to learn more about, or a project to spend time actually using. I run [yazzy](https://yazzy.jaredezz.tech) ([github](https://github.com/carterworks/yazzy)) on my homelab and use it whenever I share articles with people. They think I'm cool, but I'm really just drawing from Carter's associated coolness.

---

The blogs I actually read are the ones written by people I find interesting. And if I know them, they're so much more interesting. I'd like to do the challenge again sometime, maybe sooner rather than later, and hopefully without missing a week. Maybe even making a zine when we're done because physical print medium–atoms–are funner than bits.
