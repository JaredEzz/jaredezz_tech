## Intro

A full catalog of my Switch library — descriptions, cover art, reviews, and HowLongToBeat estimates. The top of the list is my personal ranking; below that, games are grouped by playability (singleplayer offline first, then mostly-singleplayer with optional co-op, then party / local-multiplayer titles, then primarily-online games).

**Quick stats**

- **Total unique titles:** 42 (44 entries; *Pokémon Sword* and *Breath of the Wild* are owned in both digital and physical). The *Ezio Collection* counts as one inventory entry but contains three games (AC II, Brotherhood, Revelations).
- **Digital cards:** 34 (35 with Fortnite counted twice). *Mario Kart World removed — Switch 2 only.*
- **Physical cartridges:** 10
- **Switch 2 enhanced:** *Super Mario Galaxy 1 + 2* runs on the original Switch with Switch 2 enhancements (4K, mouse controls).

**Legend:** 💾 = owned digital · 🎮 = owned physical · 💾🎮 = owned in both formats.

## ⭐ Top Ranked

Personal top picks, in priority order.

### 1. The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild 💾🎮
<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/c/c6/The_Legend_of_Zelda_Breath_of_the_Wild.jpg" alt="cover" style="max-width:200px;max-height:280px;" />
- **Players:** Single-player
- **Offline:** Yes
- **HowLongToBeat:** Main 50h · Main+Extras 97h · Completionist 188h
- **Metacritic:** 97 (Switch)
- **Description:** Open-world action-adventure where Link awakens after 100 years to explore Hyrule, solve shrine puzzles, and defeat Calamity Ganon at his own pace.
- **Reviews:** Universal acclaim as a masterclass in open-world design and emergent physics-based gameplay; minor criticisms aimed at weapon durability and frame rate dips.

### 2. Super Mario Galaxy 1 + 2 🎮
<img src="https://mario.wiki.gallery/images/thumb/8/8b/Super_Mario_Galaxy_%2B_Super_Mario_Galaxy_2_US_box_art.jpg/250px-Super_Mario_Galaxy_%2B_Super_Mario_Galaxy_2_US_box_art.jpg" alt="cover" style="max-width:200px;max-height:280px;" />
- **Players:** Single-player / Local 2P co-op (Co-Star pointer assist)
- **Offline:** Yes
- **HowLongToBeat:** Galaxy 1 — Main 14.5h · Completionist 42h; Galaxy 2 — Main ~13h · Completionist ~37h
- **Metacritic:** ~88 (Switch, compilation)
- **Description:** 2025 re-release of the two Wii 3D platformers in which Mario traverses gravity-bending mini-planets to rescue Princess Peach. Runs on the original Switch and is enhanced on Switch 2 (4K docked, Joy-Con 2 mouse controls, Assist Mode, new Storybook chapters).
- **Reviews:** Widely praised as a faithful, enhanced re-release of two all-time-great 3D platformers; minor complaints about the $40-per-game pricing and motion-control reliance on the original Switch.

### 3. Super Mario Odyssey 🎮
<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/8/8d/Super_Mario_Odyssey.jpg" alt="cover" style="max-width:200px;max-height:280px;" />
- **Players:** Single-player (local co-op available — second player controls Cappy)
- **Offline:** Yes
- **HowLongToBeat:** Main 12.5h · Main+Extras 27h · Completionist 61.5h
- **Metacritic:** 97 (Switch)
- **Description:** Open-ended 3D platformer where Mario and his sentient hat Cappy travel between distinct "kingdoms" to rescue Princess Peach, using Cappy to possess enemies and objects.
- **Reviews:** Near-universal acclaim for its inventive level design, capture mechanic, and creative variety; ranked among the greatest games ever made, with only minor criticism for an easy difficulty curve.
- **▶ Recommended:** Play *after* finishing Super Mario Galaxy 1 + 2.

### 4. Assassin's Creed: The Rebel Collection 💾
<img src="https://art.gametdb.com/switch/coverHQ/US/ATXEA.jpg" alt="cover" style="max-width:200px;max-height:280px;" />

- **Offline:** Yes
- **HowLongToBeat:** Main 37h · Main+Extras 73h · Completionist ~110h (combined Black Flag + Rogue)

- **Description:** Bundle of *Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag* and *Assassin's Creed Rogue* — open-world pirate-era naval exploration plus the franchise's only Templar-perspective campaign.
- **Reviews:** Widely praised as a near-flawless Switch port of two well-loved Caribbean/North-Atlantic adventures, with only minor complaints about Rogue being the weaker of the two games.
