## Intro

I had fun going back through my phones recently in a [blog post](/posts/fantastic-computers-and-where-to-find-them) and wanted to do the same for the computers I've worn on my wrist and the stories behind them.

But first, an aside — why do we wear stuff on our wrist? It's weird. I guess they're the flattest face easily viewable. But I feel like smart earrings or glasses should've caught on, or smart pocket watches. I don't know.

## Nike+ FuelBand

Junior high. Super cool. Didn't have a phone, so I paired it on my mom's iPad and our home PC. The USB clasp was so cool. Died in a scooter accident but saved me.
